EarlySight doesn't replace your MIS. It sits beside it, reads the data your staff already record, and shows each member of staff which of their pupils are changing.

There are two ways to get your data in.

## 1. Your MIS's own exports

Your data manager runs standard reports in your MIS, saves them as spreadsheet (CSV) files and uploads them in EarlySight's Admin area.

- **Your own column names and codes.** EarlySight shows a preview of each file. Anything it doesn't recognise, such as a behaviour type, a grade name or a year group called "Remove", is asked about once and remembered.
- **Previous years too.** Past years' attendance, behaviour and grades can be uploaded, so comparisons with last year work from the first day.
- **Upload as often as you like.** Each upload adds to what's already there.

## 2. A direct connection through Wonde

[Wonde](https://www.wonde.com/) connects school management systems to the education apps a school uses. With a Wonde connection, the school approves EarlySight's access in Wonde and can see exactly which data it reads. Data then arrives without anyone running reports.

**Subject to MIS API availability.** Not every MIS shares every kind of data through Wonde. Lesson registers, behaviour and grades vary most from one system to another. Where something isn't available through Wonde, it can still come from an export.

## What EarlySight reads

| Data | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Pupils: year group, form, SEN, FSM and Pupil Premium | Who each member of staff sees, and the groups compared |
| Lesson registers | Attendance, lesson by lesson, and [missed lessons between registers](/lesson-attendance/) |
| Behaviour and rewards | Changes in a pupil's behaviour, compared with their own record |
| Report grades and effort | Each pupil's position in each subject over time |
| Tests and assessments (optional) | Test results compared with teacher judgement |
| KS2 and exam results (optional) | Where each pupil started, and how far they've come |

The more of these a school sends, the more EarlySight can show.

## Your data stays yours

- **Read only.** EarlySight never writes anything back to your MIS.
- **Each person sees their own pupils.** Form tutors see their form, year leaders their year, senior leaders the whole school. The limits are enforced on the server, not just hidden on screen.
- **A data processing agreement** with every school, and [Cyber Essentials certified](https://registry.blockmarktech.com/certificates/4498909c-b5b0-4c9a-8c0e-a70b8dabcdb7/active/).
- **Never used elsewhere.** A school's data is never used in our demonstrations, testing, training or AI tools. See our [privacy notice](/privacy/).
