*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

## Who we are

This website is run by **Early Sight Education Ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17487881). We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

To contact us about your information, email [admin@earlysight.co.uk](mailto:admin@earlysight.co.uk).

## What this notice covers

This notice covers people who visit earlysight.co.uk, ask for our brochure, or contact us. It doesn't cover pupil and staff data that schools hold in EarlySight. For that, see [If your school uses EarlySight](#if-your-school-uses-earlysight) below.

## What we collect, and why

**When you ask for the brochure.** We collect your work email address and, if you choose to give them, your name and your school. We use them to follow up about the brochure. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to your request.

**If you tick "Send me occasional EarlySight updates".** We'll send you occasional emails about EarlySight. Our lawful basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by replying to any email from us or by emailing [admin@earlysight.co.uk](mailto:admin@earlysight.co.uk).

**When you email us**, for example to book a demo. We use your email address, and anything you choose to tell us, to reply and to arrange the demo. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry.

**When you visit the website.** We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Two services see your device's IP address because your browser connects to them to load the site:

- **GitHub Pages**, which hosts the website, keeps server logs for security.
- **Google Fonts**, which provides the website's typefaces.

## Who we share it with

We don't sell your information or share it for anyone else's marketing. It passes through these services, which handle it on our behalf:

- **FormSubmit** (formsubmit.co) sends us the brochure form by email.
- **Microsoft 365** holds our email.
- **GitHub** and **Google**, as above.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK, including in the United States.

## How long we keep it

We keep brochure requests and enquiries for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. If you withdraw consent to updates, we stop sending them straight away.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
- ask us to correct it;
- ask us to delete it;
- object to how we use it, or ask us to limit it;
- withdraw your consent to updates at any time.

To do any of these, email [admin@earlysight.co.uk](mailto:admin@earlysight.co.uk). We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or on 0303 123 1113.

## If your school uses EarlySight

When a school uses EarlySight, the school is the data controller for its pupils' and staff's information, and we process it on the school's behalf under a data processing agreement. The school's own privacy notice explains how that information is used. Questions about it should go to the school first.

A school's data is never used in our demonstrations, testing, training or AI tools. Our demonstrations use an invented school.

## Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
