## A subheading

Two hashes make a subheading. Three make a smaller one.

- A dash and a space make a bullet point
- **Two stars** make bold text; *one star* makes italics
- A link: [the DfE attendance figures](https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/pupil-absence-in-schools-in-england)
